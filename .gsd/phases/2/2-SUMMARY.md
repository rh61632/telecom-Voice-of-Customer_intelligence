## 1. Executive Summary

Plan 2.2 performed a comprehensive, read-only empirical audit across all Classical Machine Learning training pipelines in `ml/`, serialized `.joblib` models in `ml/models/`, evaluation reports in `ml/reports/`, and human gold standard datasets in `data/gold_set/` (along with `archive/scraped_data_2020/gold_set/archived_600_plan/`).

The audit empirically verified exact seed dataset counts ($N = 4,500$ across Grameenphone, Banglalink, and Robi), established the exact Google Colab origin of `category_voting_classifier.joblib` and `sentiment_voting_classifier.joblib` (pickled under scikit-learn 1.6.1, raising 34 and 28 warnings under local 1.9.1), surfaced training script bugs (`train_category.py` standard deviation duplication, `train_filtered_experiments.py` dead gold path, table-vs-prose contradictions), exposed language detector threshold contradictions (60% effective English threshold vs 70% Gambäck & Das docstring), reproduced Cohen's kappa calculations, and uncovered a **critical benchmark provenance contradiction**: project reports and benchmark tables publish metrics from an archived 600-sample dataset that archival documentation explicitly claimed was "never executed", while active 300-sample human ground-truth Macro-F1 is substantially different.

Both tasks defined in `2/2-PLAN.md` completed with passing verification commands. Zero project source, model, or data files outside `.gsd/` were modified.

- **Ground Truth Verification:** Live seed files (`data/processed/my{gp,bl,robi}_classified_reviews.csv`) total exactly **$N = 4,500$** rows (1,500 each; 0 nulls in text/sentiment/category; `operator` column missing in `mygp_classified_reviews.csv`).
  - Sentiment: `2,774 Positive / 1,238 Negative / 488 Neutral`.
  - Category: `2,731 General Appreciation / 788 Offers / 400 App Login / 364 Network / 217 Billing`.
- **Stale Count Discrepancies:**
  - `ml/README.md:23`: Cites `~10.9%` Neutral ($489 / 4499 = 10.87\%$, whereas live count is $488 / 4500 = 10.84\%$).
  - `ml/reports/category_classification_report.md:5,33,37`: Hardcodes $N = 4,499$ in prose, reports General support as `2730` (live is `2,731`), and overall accuracy support as `4499`.
  - `ml/reports/short_text_filtering_ablation_study.md:12`: Table row 1 reports Full Dataset samples as `4,499`.
  - `scripts/evaluate_linguistic_subcohorts.py:156` & `ml/reports/linguistic_subcohort_report.md:5`: Prose states $N = 4,499$ while benchmark table row 5 immediately below it reports $N = 4,500$.

### 2.2 Serialized Model Provenance & Version Inconsistencies
- **Colab Packaging Origin:** Deserializing all 9 models in `ml/models/` under scikit-learn 1.9.1 revealed that `category_voting_classifier.joblib` (34 warnings) and `sentiment_voting_classifier.joblib` (28 warnings) were serialized with scikit-learn **1.6.1**.
- **Pipeline Structure Provenance Discrepancy:**
  - `ml/train_category.py:84-133` specifies `char_wb` n-grams (3–5), `min_df=3`, `max_features=None`, `LogisticRegression(C=1.0)`, `ComplementNB(norm=True, alpha=1.0)`, and soft voting weights `[2, 2, 1]`.
  - `ml/models/category_voting_classifier.joblib` on disk contains `char` n-grams (3–5), `min_df=2`, `max_features=25000`, `word` n-grams `max_features=12000`, `LogisticRegression(C=2.5)`, `ComplementNB(norm=False, alpha=0.5)`, and soft voting weights `[2.0, 2.5, 1.0]`.
  - **Empirical Proof:** This exact pipeline matches lines 387–436 and Cell 7 of [`dl/telecom_voc_master_unified_colab.ipynb`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/dl/telecom_voc_master_unified_colab.ipynb#L387-L436), proving the model on disk was imported from Google Colab rather than generated by the local training script.
  - In contrast, `category_logistic_regression.joblib` and `category_linearsvc.joblib` match `ml/train_category.py` (`char_wb`, `min_df=3`, `max_features=None`, `sklearn 1.9.1`).
- **Negative Sentence Prediction Divergence:** On `"Network speed khub baje, taka kete niyeche"`, while full-corpus models classified it as Negative (51.42% Voting, 53.04% LR), `sentiment_ensemble_filtered_min3words.joblib` misclassified it as **Positive** (`Positive 41.10%` vs `Negative 40.58%`).

### 2.3 Training Script Bugs & Reporting Inconsistencies
- **`ml/train_category.py:247` Formatting Bug:** Code repeats `m['mean_macro_f1']` instead of printing standard deviation (`{m['mean_macro_f1']:.4f} ± {m['mean_macro_f1']:.4f}`). The committed report [`category_classification_report.md:11-25`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/ml/reports/category_classification_report.md#L11-L25) contains valid standard deviations (`± 0.0190`) and an external Google Colab GPU benchmark table, proving the report was manually assembled and would be corrupted if `train_category.py` were re-run.
- **`ml/train_filtered_experiments.py:19` Dead Gold Path:** Sets `GOLD_CSV` to `scraped_data_2020/gold_set/gold_set_verified.csv` (which was moved to `archive/`). Because the path does not exist, running the script silently returns `0.0, 0.0`.
- **Ablation Study Internal Contradiction:** [`ml/reports/short_text_filtering_ablation_study.md`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/ml/reports/short_text_filtering_ablation_study.md) table reports Gold Set Macro-F1 rising from **`0.6737` to `0.7318`** ($\mathbf{+5.81\%}$), whereas the narrative prose asserts a jump from **`0.6781` to `0.7436`** ($\mathbf{+6.55\% \text{ to } +7.80\%}$). The prose hardcoded metrics from character-only `train_ensemble.py` on the archived 600-sample gold set rather than the table's FeatureUnion pipeline. Lines 19 and 37 also have malformed LaTeX `(≥ 3$ words)`.
- **Linguistic Subcohort Omission:** [`scripts/evaluate_linguistic_subcohorts.py:104-109`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/scripts/evaluate_linguistic_subcohorts.py#L104-L109) excludes the 14 `Symbolic / Emoticons` reviews from the subcohort rows while including all 4,500 reviews in Overall CV ($1,091 + 390 + 3,005 = 4,486 \ne 4,500$). Furthermore, line 81 stratifies cross-validation folds on `y_cat` for both category and sentiment tasks.
- **Language Detector Threshold Contradiction:** [`ml/language_detector.py:6,86,153`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/ml/language_detector.py#L6) cites Gambäck & Das (2016) with a $\ge 70\%$ dominance threshold, but line 153 falls through to `elif english_ratio >= 0.60: lang = "English"` whenever `banglish_matches == 0`, lowering the effective English threshold to 60%.
- **Global Language Date-Range Mismatch:** [`ml/reports/global_language_distribution.md:3`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/ml/reports/global_language_distribution.md#L3) claims the analysis covers `(2016–2026)`, while [`global_language_distribution.json:90-152`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/ml/reports/global_language_distribution.json#L90-L152) and the full corpus cover `2014–2026` (including 183 reviews in 2014 and 910 in 2015).

### 2.4 Critical Gold Standard Benchmark Provenance Contradiction
- **The Archival Disclaimer:** [`archive/scraped_data_2020/gold_set/archived_600_plan/README.md:3,11,16`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/archive/scraped_data_2020/gold_set/archived_600_plan/README.md#L3) explicitly claims that the exploratory 600-sample evaluation plan was *"discarded and never executed"*, that manual annotation for 600 reviews was *"never performed"*, and that all project reports reference the authentic $N = 300$ / $N = 200$ human ground truth.
- **The Empirical Reality:** Controlled read-only model evaluation proved that the benchmark leaderboard, the per-operator breakdown ($200 + 200 + 200 = 600$), and the confusion matrix ($76 \text{ Neg} + 72 \text{ Neu} + 452 \text{ Pos} = 600$) in [`data/gold_set/comprehensive_benchmark_table.md:30-36`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/data/gold_set/comprehensive_benchmark_table.md#L30-L36) and [`data/gold_set/gold_standard_evaluation_report.md:18-58`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/data/gold_set/gold_standard_evaluation_report.md#L18-L58) originate **100% from the archived 600-sample `gold_set_verified.csv`**:
  - On archived `g600`: `ensemble_voting_classifier.joblib` achieves **`82.00%` Acc / `0.6781` Macro-F1**; `challenger_linearsvc.joblib` achieves **`79.33%` / `0.6305`**; `primary_logistic_regression.joblib` achieves **`75.33%` / `0.6347`** (matching published tables to four decimal places).
  - On active `g300` (`data/gold_set/blind_annotation_300_annotator1.csv`, $N=300$): `ensemble_voting_classifier.joblib` achieves **`89.00%` Acc / `0.6089` Macro-F1**; `sentiment_voting_classifier.joblib` achieves **`83.67%` / `0.5625`**; `challenger_linearsvc.joblib` achieves **`89.00%` / `0.6330`**; `category_voting_classifier.joblib` achieves **`91.33%` / `0.4919`**.
- **Conclusion:** Published project reports claim to evaluate models against an authentic $N=300$ human ground truth while actually presenting numbers from the supposedly "discarded/never-executed" 600-sample dataset. On the true $N=300$ ground truth, model Macro-F1 drops from 0.6781 to 0.6089.

### 2.5 Inter-Annotator Agreement, Documentation Links & Rulebook Defects
- **Cohen's Kappa Verification:** Re-running `calculate_agreement.py` confirmed Sentiment raw agreement of **91.00%** ($\kappa = 0.5762$) and Operational Category agreement of **95.41%** ($\kappa = 0.7694$ on $N=196$). Per-operator metrics match `comprehensive_benchmark_table.md:20-22` (GP `89.55% / 0.6921`, BL `94.03% / 0.4846`, Robi `89.39% / 0.3400`).
- **De-Anonymization:** `calculate_agreement.py:77-78,154,188` prints `"Ratul"` and `"Friend"` instead of generic annotator identifiers.
- **Missing File Links:** `data/README.md:23-24`, `comprehensive_benchmark_table.md:42-43`, and `gold_standard_evaluation_report.md:14,91` link to missing `blind_annotation_300_ratul.csv` and `blind_annotation_200_friend.csv` (which exist on disk as `_annotator1` and `_annotator2`).
- **Broken Relative Directory Link:** `gold_standard_evaluation_report.md:14` links to `archived_600_plan/README.md` (which does not exist inside `data/gold_set/`).
- **Rulebook Violation on `[BLIND_106]`:** In `blind_annotation_300_annotator1.csv`, review `[BLIND_106]` (`"Good but price is some high"`) was categorized as `Billing & Airtime Deductions`, violating [`ANNOTATION_RULEBOOK.md:122`](file:///c:/Users/user/Ratul/project/telecom-Voice-of-Customer_intelligence/data/gold_set/ANNOTATION_RULEBOOK.md#L122) which requires price complaints to be classified under `Offers & Data Packs`.
- **LaTeX Formatting Syntax Errors:** `data/README.md:25` and `gold_standard_evaluation_report.md:139` write `κ = 0.7694$` and `κ = 0.5762$` without opening `$` symbols.
