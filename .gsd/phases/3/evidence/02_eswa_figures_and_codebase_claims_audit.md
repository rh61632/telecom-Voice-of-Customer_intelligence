## 1. Embedded Figures Visual Defects & Caption Alignment

### 1.1 Hardcoded Burned-In Numbering Offset Defect
The active manuscript includes five figure environments. When `figure0_system_architecture.png` was added at line 94 as the first figure, LaTeX assigned it **Figure 1** (`\caption{End-to-End Architectural Pipeline of the VoC-Intelligence Decision Support System...}`). Consequently, LaTeX dynamically assigned subsequent numbers:
- `figure1_monthly_nss_trajectory.png` (line 389) $\to$ **LaTeX Figure 2**
- `figure2_yearly_leadership_strip.png` (line 431) $\to$ **LaTeX Figure 3**
- `figure3_priority_matrix.png` (line 482) $\to$ **LaTeX Figure 4**
- `figure4_banglalink_reversal_decomposition.png` (line 500) $\to$ **LaTeX Figure 5**

However, the raster PNG image assets themselves were generated with **hardcoded header titles** burned into the pixel bitmaps by `scripts/generate_paper_figures.py`:
- `figure1` bitmap header: `"Figure 1: Ten-Year Monthly Net Sentiment Score (NSS) Trajectory..."`
- `figure2` bitmap header: `"Figure 2: Annual Voice-of-Customer Leadership Strip (2016–2026..."`
- `figure3` bitmap header: `"Figure 3: Strategic VoC Priority Matrix (Prevalence vs Dissatisfaction..."`
- `figure4` bitmap header: `"Figure 4: Additive Domain Decomposition of Banglalink's 2025–2026 NSS Reversal..."`

In the compiled PDF (both `manuscript.pdf` and `manuscript_twocolumn.pdf`), this causes an egregious double-numbering clash on every single page where figures appear:
- The LaTeX caption below the image reads: **"Figure 2: Decade-Long Longitudinal Monthly Net Sentiment Score..."**
- While inside the image itself at the top, the bold title reads: **"Figure 1: Ten-Year Monthly Net Sentiment Score..."**
- This $+1$ offset persists across Figures 2, 3, 4, and 5.

### 1.2 Figure 4 Header Title Edge Truncation / Clipping
- **Asset:** `paper/eswa_latex/figures/figure4_banglalink_reversal_decomposition.png` (Dimensions: $2850 \times 1560$ px).
- **Generation Logic:** `scripts/generate_paper_figures.py:346`:
  `plt.title('Figure 4: Additive Domain Decomposition of Banglalink\'s 2025–2026 NSS Reversal (+15.54 Points)', pad=14, fontweight='bold', fontsize=12)`
- **Empirical Pixel Analysis:**
  - Measuring dark text pixels in the top 10% of image height reveals a text span from $x = 45$ to $x = 2849$ across a canvas of width $2850$.
  - Right margin: **1 pixel**.
  - Because `plt.tight_layout()` does not account for overly wide figure titles on narrow figure canvases, the closing parenthesis and final letter of `Points)` hit the boundary, resulting in visual clipping where `"(+15.54 Point"` cuts off `"s)"`.

### 1.3 Strategic Priority Matrix (Figure 3) Quadrant Prevalence Denominator Mismatch
In Section 5.3 (lines 490–493), the narrative prose explains the quadrant placement of operational categories:
- Line 491: `"App Login & Technical Bugs (Quadrant I): Combines high prevalence (4.8% to 11.5%) with severe negativity (87.1% to 89.3%)... cementing its status as the primary urgent crisis driver."`
- Line 493: `"Offers & Data Packs (Quadrant IV): High prevalence (11.5% to 24.0%) but lowest negativity (50.3% to 56.6%)..."`

However, in `figure3_priority_matrix.png` (generated by `scripts/generate_paper_figures.py:216–263`):
- Quadrant dividing lines are set at **Prevalence $= 4.5\%$** and **Negativity $= 75.0\%$**:
  - Quadrant I (Urgent Crisis, Top Right): $\text{Prevalence} > 4.5\%$, $\text{Negativity} > 75.0\%$
  - Quadrant II (Severe Latent Churn, Top Left): $\text{Prevalence} \le 4.5\%$, $\text{Negativity} > 75.0\%$
  - Quadrant III (Infrastructure Parity, Bottom Left): $\text{Prevalence} \le 4.5\%$, $\text{Negativity} \le 75.0\%$
  - Quadrant IV (Feature Optimization, Bottom Right): $\text{Prevalence} > 4.5\%$, $\text{Negativity} \le 75.0\%$
- **The Empirical Conflict:**
  1. **Grameenphone App Login & Bugs** has a plotted prevalence of **$3.88\%$** and negativity of $88.95\%$. Because $3.88\% < 4.5\%$, **GP App Login is placed inside QUADRANT II**, directly contradicting the prose assertion that all App Login complaints fall in Quadrant I ($4.8\%–11.5\%$).
  2. **Banglalink Offers & Packs** has a plotted prevalence of **$4.31\%$** and negativity of $56.13\%$. Because $4.31\% < 4.5\%$, **BL Offers is placed inside QUADRANT III**, directly contradicting the prose assertion that all Offers fall in Quadrant IV ($11.5\%–24.0\%$).
- **Root Cause:** The prose calculated prevalence as a percentage within functional operational complaints ($N = 88{,}181$), yielding $4.8\%–11.5\%$ for App Login and $11.5\%–24.0\%$ for Offers. The plotting script calculated prevalence as a percentage of each operator's total multi-year review volume ($N = 655{,}649$), compressing the x-coordinates down to $0.78\%–8.12\%$ and shoving GP App Login and BL Offers across the $4.5\%$ threshold into adjacent quadrants.


### 2.2 Fabricated Preprocessing & Banglish Lemmatization
- **Manuscript Claim (lines 112–120):**  
  `"Bengali-specific Unicode normalization stripping zero-width non-joiners (ZWNJ, U+200C) and zero-width joiners (ZWJ, U+200D)... morpho-phonetic Banglish lemmatizer that normalizes phonetic spelling permutations (e.g., 'faltu', 'fal2', 'faaltu' -> 'faltu')."`
- **Codebase Reality:** An exhaustive regex search across all preprocessing and tokenization scripts in `ml/`, `dl/`, and `scraped_data_global/` demonstrates:
  - Occurrences of `ZWNJ` / `\u200c` / `\u200d`: **0**
  - Occurrences of `fal2` / `faaltu` / Banglish lemmatizer: **0**
- **Discrepancy:** The text-cleaning pipeline in `ml/train_category.py` and `scraped_data_global/pipeline/` uses basic lowercase conversion, punctuation stripping, and standard whitespace splitting. Zero Unicode zero-width character handling or phonetic lemmatizers exist anywhere in the repository.

### 2.3 BiLSTM Neural Architecture Fiction
- **Manuscript Claim (line 284 & Figure 0):**  
  Table 2 row 6 lists: `GloVe (300d) + Char-CNN` with `Hybrid BiLSTM + Attn`. Figure 0 illustrates `BiLSTM with GloVe 300d embeddings and multi-head attention`.
- **Codebase Reality (`dl/train_bilstm.py:113–155`):**
  - Word embedding layer: `self.word_embedding = nn.Embedding(word_vocab_size, word_emb_dim=128, padding_idx=0)`. It initializes randomly generated 128-dimensional PyTorch embeddings. No pretrained GloVe weights (300d or otherwise) are loaded or referenced.
  - Attention layer: `class Attention(nn.Module)` implements a single-head additive (Bahdanau-style) self-attention projection (`self.proj = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, hidden_dim)`, `self.query = nn.Linear(hidden_dim, 1, bias=False)`).
- **Discrepancy:** GloVe 300d embeddings and multi-head attention are completely fictional claims not supported by the neural architecture in code.
