### Decision 1: Excel Workbook Data Source, Directional Driver Rule & Cached Values
**Question:** How should `scripts/build_paper_empirical_excel.py` compute trajectories, Proposition 2 contingency counts, and `Model_Bias_and_Ablation`, and should Excel formulas be written?
- **Data source mismatch:** Currently `build_paper_empirical_excel.py:71-80` loads the 3 `my*_classified_global.csv` files and filters `<= "2026-09-30 23:59:59"`, yielding **634,998** modern rows instead of **634,992** in `all_operators_common_duration_classified.csv` (6 extra rows from `2026-09-30 23:24:37..23:51:40`, after the synchronized `common_window_end = 2026-09-30 23:16:00`).

This causes **40/240 transitions** to pick the wrong leading domain and inflates `Null_Comparison_Prop2` Non-Shock App Login leads from **91/205** to **96/205**!
- **Openpyxl Formula Caching:** `openpyxl` does **not** calculate or store cached values when writing Excel formulas (`=SUM(...)`); any reader using `openpyxl(..., data_only=True)` or `pandas.read_excel()` sees `None`/`NaN` in formula cells unless opened and saved in Excel/LibreOffice.
- **Decision:** Read directly from `all_operators_common_duration_classified.csv` (`ym >= "2020-01"` -> exact 634,992 rows), compute transitions/deltas/leading drivers on unrounded floats before formatting for display, compute `Model_Bias_and_Ablation` dynamically from active Gold $N=300$ (`Gold_NSS = 78.33`) + live `.joblib` models + `paper_empirical_ground_truth.json`, and write pre-evaluated numeric values (no unevaluated formula strings that break `data_only=True` readers).
