2. HUMAN GOLD STANDARD EXPANSION & COHEN'S KAPPA AGREEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Context:
  Currently verified on initial human ground truth. A multi-reviewer protocol
  is planned to establish publication-grade inter-annotator reliability.

Action Plan:
  - Add an independent second annotator for a blind review sample (N = 300-600).
  - Calculate Cohen's Kappa score (inter-rater agreement metric) using:
    `archive/scraped_data_2020/gold_set/calculate_agreement.py`.
  - Resolve disputed edge cases (sarcasm, star-rating mismatch, ambiguous Banglish)

3. FILTERED VS. UNFILTERED MODEL DEPLOYMENT RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Context:
  Ablation study (`ml/reports/short_text_filtering_ablation_study.md`) proved:
  - Filtered model (>=3 words, dropping 1-2 word noise) jumped from 0.6737 to
    0.7318 Macro-F1 (+5.81% F1) on human edge cases.
  - Full-dataset model (N = 4,499) has higher cross-validation accuracy (87.44%
    vs 85.98%) due to trivial easy reviews ("Good", "valo").
  - The 398k production corpus currently uses the full-dataset model.

Action Plan:
  - Once the expanded Gold Standard v2 (with Cohen's Kappa) is completed:
    Evaluate both models on the new gold ground truth.
  - Choose between:
    (a) Re-classifying the 398k corpus with the filtered model for better
        precision on actionable categories (Billing, App Bugs).
    (b) Formally publishing the trade-off in the paper/report: explaining why
        the full model was kept for inference coverage while documenting the
        filtered model's edge-case advantage.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Context:
